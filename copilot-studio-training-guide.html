Settings, orchestration & models

Why it matters: The Settings page is where the agent's "brain" is configured. Understand it so defaults are a choice, not an accident.
fig 7 · model choice is a trade-off, not a right answer

You'll learn

  • Preview and experimental model tags: fine to explore, never for production, and your admin controls whether they appear at all

Course house rules

One agent, built progressively

Every module adds a layer to the same practice agent. By Module 10 you've published something real.

Environments come first

Always confirm you're in Dev before touching anything. It's the check that prevents the most painful mistakes.

Descriptions are a skill

The agent routes by reading your descriptions of knowledge, tools, topics, and child agents. Write them like you mean it.

Nothing ships untested

Evaluation before publishing, every time. A 10-question test set is the minimum bar.

Preview means not for production

Preview and experimental features are for exploring. If it has a preview tag, it stays out of anything real.

Ask early, ask often

Post questions in the shared FAQ. Your question improves the course for the next cohort.
